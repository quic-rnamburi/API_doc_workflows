◆ Modes

enum Modes

Represents the operational modes based on the device's display state.

Certain system resources are optimized only when the device display is active, primarily to conserve power. However, for critical components, tuning may be performed regardless of the display state, including during doze mode.

Enumerator
MODE_RESUME 

Tuning allowed when the display is on.

MODE_SUSPEND 

Tuning allowed when the display is off.

MODE_DOZE 

Tuning allowed during doze (low-power idle) mode.
