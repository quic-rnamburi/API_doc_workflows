#define VCPM_PARAM_ID_SAFETY_MARGIN   0x08001B34

Identifier for the parameter that sets the safety margin. This shall be considered as an aggregated margin for the containers in the SubGraph. It will be absorbed by the VCPM sitting in the master domain to calculate the packet delivery timelines.
