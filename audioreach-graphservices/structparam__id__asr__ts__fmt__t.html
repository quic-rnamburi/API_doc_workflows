uint32_t param_id_asr_ts_fmt_t::reset_period

@h2xmle_description {Indicates the period to read reference UTC time.

Note: '0' means module latches to UTC time only at the beginning of the usecase and doesn't periodically sync to UTC time for dynamic changes. For non-zero values say 'x' seconds, the module will dynamically sync and update the UTC time at every x seconds. The maximum reset period allowed is a day.} @h2xmle_default {0} @h2xmle_range {0..86400}
