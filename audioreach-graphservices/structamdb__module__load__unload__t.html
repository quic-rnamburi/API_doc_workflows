Detailed Description

Payload for AMDB_CMD_LOAD_MODULES, AMDB_CMD_RSP_LOAD_MODULES, and AMDB_CMD_UNLOAD_MODULES.

The overall payload can be for both in-band and out-of-band data.

  • For an in-band payload, the incoming payload is copied, modified (if necessary), and sent in the response.
  • For an out-of-band payload, the incoming payload is sent in the response.
