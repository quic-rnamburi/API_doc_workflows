◆ maxsinkBufTws

uint32_t param_id_aptx_adaptive_enc_init_t::maxsinkBufTws

@h2xmle_description {Informs the aptX adaptive Encoder of the minimum & maximum buffering,in mS, that
the A2DP Sink can accept.


These are the minimum/maximum buffering needed from reception of the packet by the sink to the rendering of the first audio sample of that packet
For minsinkBufxx, each bit represents 1 mS of audio
For maxsinkBufxx, each bit represents 4 mS of audio
minsinkBufxx values default to 20 (20mS)
maxsinkBufxx values default to 50 (200mS)
A value of 0 means "unchanged". }

@h2xmle_range {0..256} @h2xmle_default {20}
